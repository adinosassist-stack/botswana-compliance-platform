# Thebe Property Portfolio & Professional Valuation v1

Property Portfolio v1 turns the existing Property Intelligence scenario calculator into a governed business record.

## What becomes authoritative

- A tenant-scoped property and land register.
- Owner-recorded acquisition cost, rent, operating-cost and debt fields.
- An immutable history of professional valuation reports recorded in Thebe.
- Portfolio coverage, debt and operating summaries that feed Business Analytics.

## Professional valuation boundary

Thebe does **not** create, certify or sign a property market valuation. A market value enters the authoritative analytics layer only when an owner records an externally signed professional report with:

- valuation date;
- market value;
- valuer name;
- valuer registration reference; and
- report reference.

The recorded valuation row is immutable. A later professional report supersedes it analytically by date; history remains preserved.

## AI and automation boundary

The AI may explain recorded figures, compare scenarios and identify missing or stale professional valuation coverage. It may not invent comparable sales, promote an underwriting scenario into market value, sign a report, or represent itself as the registered valuer.

## Product role

Property & Valuation is a specialist vertical feeding the same Owner Command Centre and Analytics layer used by Money, Sales, Operations and Compliance. This keeps one governed business model rather than creating a separate property-intelligence stack.
