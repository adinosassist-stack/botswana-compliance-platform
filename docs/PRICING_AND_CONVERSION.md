# Pricing & Conversion

Current UI pricing:
- Starter — P99/month
- Business — P249/month
- Business Pro — P499/month

New workspaces receive the configured trial period (default 14 days).

The `plan` field stores a plan preference/entitlement target only. It does not constitute payment. Production billing should use hosted checkout or tokenized provider flows; raw card data must never enter BW Compliance OS.

Professional legal, tax, accounting, company-secretarial or reviewer services are separate from SaaS subscription pricing unless explicitly bundled later.
