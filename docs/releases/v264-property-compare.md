# V264 Property Compare

Property gains a compact `Compare` workspace for side-by-side, input-driven asset comparison.

## Scope

- Adds `Compare` to the Property primary workspace navigation and Today quick actions.
- Uses the current deal plus up to two alternatives entered by the user.
- Compares purchase price, monthly rent, annual rent and gross yield only from entered values.
- Shows objective signals for lowest entered price, highest entered rent and highest entered gross yield without selecting an investment outcome.
- Keeps alternative fields blank by default; no fabricated comparable properties or live-market claims are introduced.
- Labels the workspace as neither a live market feed nor an investment recommendation.
- Adds grounded Property AI actions for trade-off explanation and deeper-analysis input preparation.
- Preserves compact Property calculator, Operations, Optimise, full-screen Property AI, mobile overflow protection and DOM-safety constraints.
