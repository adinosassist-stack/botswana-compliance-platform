# Privacy Notice — Review Draft

**Status: DRAFT — obtain Botswana legal/privacy review before publication.**

BW Compliance OS processes business-account information and, when customers choose to use workforce/evidence features, may process personal information relating to employees, directors, shareholders, beneficial owners, professional reviewers and other contacts.

## What we collect
Depending on enabled features: account/contact information, company profile information, compliance records, employee-reference metadata, audit/security records, document metadata, uploaded evidence files, support communications and technical/security information.

## Why we use it
To provide the compliance service; authenticate users; maintain tenant security; calculate and track applicable compliance tasks; generate reports; maintain evidence/audit history; provide professional-review workflows; operate support/security; meet legal obligations; and protect the service from abuse.

## Sensitive evidence
Customers should upload only information necessary for a compliance purpose. Access to evidence is role-scoped. Production evidence is intended to be held in private encrypted object storage and accessed through short-lived authorized links.

## Sharing
Information may be shared with infrastructure/subprocessor providers strictly as necessary to operate the service, and with professional reviewers only where the customer has authorized that workflow. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

## Retention
Retention periods should be defined by record category, customer instructions, legal obligations and dispute/audit needs. Deleted customer data should move through a controlled deletion process including backup-expiry rules.

## Security
Controls include authenticated access, server-side tenant boundaries, encryption in transit, private evidence storage, access logging, rate limiting, backup controls and incident-response procedures. No system is completely risk-free.

## Individual rights and requests
Create a documented process for access, correction, objection/restriction, deletion or other rights that apply under Botswana data-protection law. Verify identity before disclosing personal information.

## Contact / regulator
Before launch, insert the operator's legal entity, registered/business address, privacy contact, support contact and any required Information and Data Protection Commission details.

## Changes
Material privacy-notice changes should be versioned, dated and communicated appropriately.
