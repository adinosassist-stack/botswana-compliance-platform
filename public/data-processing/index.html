Data Processing Terms

This page summarises the data-processing commitments Thebe Desk intends to apply when processing customer-submitted personal information on behalf of a business customer.

Last updated: 14 October 2026
Contracting note. These public terms are a transparency baseline. Where a customer requires a signed Data Processing Agreement, the signed agreement and verified legal-entity details should control if they differ from this page.
On this pageRolesInstructionsSecuritySubprocessorsRights supportIncidentsDeletionTransfers

1. Processing roles

For customer information entered into a Thebe Desk workspace, the business customer generally determines why and how that information is used for its operations. Thebe Desk processes that information to provide and secure the service, subject to the applicable agreement and law.

2. Customer instructions

Thebe Desk will process customer-submitted information to provide contracted product functions, maintain security, provide support, prevent abuse and meet lawful obligations. Customers are responsible for having an appropriate basis to provide the information to the service.

3. Security measures

Thebe Desk's public implementation includes role-aware access, HTTPS-oriented production configuration, content-security controls, rate limiting, private production object-storage requirements, malware-scanning requirements for uploaded evidence, audit records and account-deletion controls. See Security & Trust.

4. Subprocessors

Thebe Desk may use subprocessors where needed to provide the service. Publicly verified providers and unresolved provider categories are maintained on the Subprocessor Register. Thebe Desk should update that register when a material processor changes.

5. Data-subject and customer requests

Thebe Desk provides account export and deletion functionality and will use reasonable efforts to help customers respond to lawful access, correction, deletion or similar requests where the relevant information is processed through the service.

6. Security incidents

If Thebe Desk confirms a security incident affecting customer personal information, it should notify affected customers without undue delay and provide information reasonably available about the nature of the incident, affected information and mitigation steps, subject to legal and security constraints.

7. Return and deletion

Customers may request account deletion through available controls. Deletion can be delayed by an active legal hold or other lawful retention requirement. Backup and residual-system deletion timing is not represented here as a fixed contractual period until those operational details are formally verified.

8. Data location and international transfers

Thebe Desk does not currently make a public contractual claim about a specific hosting country or data-residency region on this page. Any cross-border-transfer mechanism or region-specific commitment required for a customer should be documented in the signed agreement once verified.

9. Confidentiality

People authorised to operate or support the service should access customer information only where needed for their role and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

10. Audit and assurance

Thebe Desk does not currently claim SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification. Reasonable customer diligence requests should be handled through support, with sensitive infrastructure information shared only where appropriate.

11. Contact

For DPA or privacy diligence requests, use WhatsApp +267 77 723 273 and request a privacy or contracting escalation.
