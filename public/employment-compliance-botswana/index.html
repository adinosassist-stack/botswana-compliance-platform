Botswana employment compliance guide

Employment compliance for Botswana SMEs: build the evidence trail before a dispute starts.

Employment risk rarely begins with one dramatic event. It grows when contracts, leave, warnings, performance records, grievances and decisions are scattered across managers, branches and WhatsApp chats. Thebe Desk gives SMEs a structured way to record the process and evidence while keeping employee reporting separate from leadership information.

Practical boundary: Thebe Desk is compliance-management software. It does not replace current official guidance or professional legal, tax or accounting advice.

What a defensible employment workflow needs

  • Keep employment records and case chronology connected to the employee and the specific issue being managed.
  • Use evidence links to show which approved records supported a warning, review or decision instead of relying only on a manager’s narrative.
  • Escalate high-impact or uncertain decisions for professional review rather than presenting software output as a legal determination.
  • Preserve role boundaries: employee reporters should see only their own reporting form and assigned location, not leadership dashboards or other employees’ records.

Daily reporting is operational evidence too

  • For SMEs with several locations, a short structured daily report can reveal attendance, operational incidents, sales or service issues and unresolved blockers before they become management surprises.
  • AI summaries can help leadership identify patterns, but the source reports should remain available so a summary never replaces the underlying evidence.
  • Reporter access should be token-scoped to the employee and location and should never become an alternative route into the main workspace.

What the system does not decide

  • The platform does not determine whether a dismissal, warning or other employment decision is lawful or fair. It organises facts, controls, chronology and evidence.
  • Botswana labour rules and procedures can change. High-impact decisions should be checked against current official guidance and appropriate professional advice.
  • The strongest value is preparedness: management can see missing records and unresolved actions before a dispute forces the business to reconstruct history.

Check current official guidance
