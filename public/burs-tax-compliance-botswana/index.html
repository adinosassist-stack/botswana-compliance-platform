Botswana tax compliance guide

BURS compliance for Botswana SMEs: track the right tax facts without guessing.

Tax compliance software becomes dangerous when it converts incomplete business data into confident tax conclusions. Thebe Desk is designed to keep recurring BURS work visible while separating confirmed taxpayer facts from assumptions. Annual turnover is not treated as a substitute for taxable supplies, and unresolved official-source conflicts stop automated conclusions.

Practical boundary: Thebe Desk is compliance-management software. It does not replace current official guidance or professional legal, tax or accounting advice.

Start with confirmed taxpayer facts

  • Record whether the business is registered for the relevant tax and keep the registration category or filing cycle only when it has been confirmed.
  • Keep taxable-supplies information separate from general turnover. Those figures may serve different purposes and should not silently overwrite each other.
  • Keep PAYE status and relevant payroll context explicit rather than inferring employer obligations from a generic employee count.
  • Use dates and filing periods as operational reminders, not as proof that a return was filed or a payment was made.

Why fail-closed tax logic matters

  • Official guidance can change, transitional rules can apply, and two authoritative sources can temporarily appear inconsistent. A compliance system should surface that conflict and require confirmation rather than choosing the more convenient answer.
  • When a tax fact is unknown, the safer UI state is “not confirmed”, not zero and not “not applicable”. This distinction prevents missing information from becoming a false conclusion.
  • Tax evidence should show what was relied on: registration records, filings, payment evidence or professional confirmation where appropriate.

How the workspace supports recurring BURS work

  • Tax details are owner-controlled because they contain financial and registration facts that ordinary operational roles do not need.
  • Recurring obligations can surface in Work & deadlines, while detailed tax facts remain in the tax area instead of being duplicated across the business profile.
  • The platform can flag unresolved facts and source conflicts, but it does not replace an accountant, tax adviser or current BURS guidance.

Check current official guidance

Rules and service requirements can change. Before acting on a filing, tax, licence, labour or tender requirement, verify the current official source.
