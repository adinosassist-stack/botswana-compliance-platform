Questions Botswana SMEs ask

What Thebe Desk does—and what it does not do.

Clear boundaries matter in compliance software. These answers explain how the workspace is designed to be used.

What is Thebe Desk?

It is Botswana SME compliance and operational-risk software. It organises recurring obligations, deadlines, licences, employment controls, tenders and supporting evidence into one role-aware workspace.

Can it track CIPA annual returns and company records?

Yes. The system can track CIPA-related annual-return timing and company-record tasks using confirmed business facts, while preserving the evidence used to complete them.

How does it handle BURS VAT and PAYE information?

Tax conclusions use explicitly confirmed tax facts. The platform does not substitute annual turnover for taxable supplies, and unresolved conflicts in official guidance block automated threshold conclusions.

Does it replace an accountant or lawyer?

No. It is a compliance-management system, not a substitute for professional legal, tax or accounting advice. High-impact or uncertain matters can be routed for human review.

Can it help with Botswana tenders?

Yes. Tender Control focuses on bid-specific requirements, closing dates, mandatory evidence and reusable proof. It is not a tender-writing guarantee or a substitute for checking the issuing authority's current requirements.

Is employee and reviewer access separated from leadership information?

Yes. Workspace access is role-specific and API authorization is enforced server-side. Employee reporting uses a separate scoped reporting link rather than exposing the leadership workspace.

Defensible, not merely convenient

The system is designed to prove what happened—and know when automation must stop.

Source conflicts are surfaced, high-impact employment and regulatory decisions are review-gated, evidence has integrity status, and every important action can be traced back to a company obligation or approved source.

Root-cause closure

Close corrective action

Corrective closure is allowed only after the underlying leadership intervention itself is closed. A new recurrence after closure will require a new corrective action.

Closure starts monitoring; it does not prove effectiveness. Leadership can call the systemic problem stabilized only after the monitoring period ends, the recurring-pattern counters have not advanced, and effectiveness evidence is explicitly verified.
Preventive effectiveness

Verify preventive effectiveness

A completed preventive task is not proof of recovery. Verify that the drift stopped through the full observation period.

Warning clears only after a passed verification. The server rechecks live drift counters and the observation deadline. Any new drift fails this prevention check; repeated drift still triggers full relapse.
Effectiveness verification

Verify corrective-action effectiveness

Verify whether the root-cause control actually worked. Passing is blocked before the monitoring period ends and whenever the recurring-pattern counters advanced.

Effectiveness ≠ implementation. Evidence that a control was created is not enough; this decision should be based on observed results during the monitoring period.
