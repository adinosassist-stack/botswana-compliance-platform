AI Data Use

Thebe AI is designed to help users understand business information and decide what needs attention next. This page explains the current public boundaries around AI-assisted processing.

Last updated: 14 October 2026
Provider transparency status. The specific production AI model/provider, processing region, prompt-retention settings and model-training terms are not yet publicly verified here. Customers should not infer those details. This page will be updated when those facts are formally published.

1. What Thebe AI is for

Thebe AI is an assistive layer for tasks such as explaining workspace information, identifying areas that may need attention, helping organise work and supporting business questions. It is not presented as a lawyer, accountant, tax authority or autonomous decision-maker.

2. What information AI may receive

AI features may process information a user deliberately types, speaks, selects or otherwise submits to the AI experience, together with limited workspace context needed to answer the request where that feature is enabled.

Thebe Desk should minimise the amount of workspace information sent to an AI service to what is necessary for the requested feature.

3. Human review

High-impact business, employment, compliance, financial, legal and tax decisions remain the user's responsibility. AI output can be wrong, incomplete or outdated. Thebe Desk is designed to support human review rather than silently automate consequential decisions.

4. Model training and retention

Thebe Desk does not make an unsupported public claim about whether a third-party AI provider may retain prompts or use content for model improvement. Before sensitive production use, the applicable provider configuration and contractual terms should be documented on this page and in the Subprocessor Register.

5. AI providers and cross-border processing

No specific production AI provider or processing country is asserted on this page until verified. If an external AI processor is used for customer workspace information, Thebe Desk will identify the provider, purpose and relevant processing information in its public subprocessor disclosure.

6. Sensitive information

Until provider-specific retention, training and residency settings are publicly documented, customers should avoid placing highly sensitive personal information, credentials, identity numbers, bank-access secrets or other information not required for the task into AI prompts.

7. Voice features

Where voice interaction is enabled, audio or speech-derived text may be processed to provide the requested interaction. The exact speech/AI provider and retention configuration must be disclosed before it is represented as a verified customer commitment.

8. Changes and questions

Material changes to AI providers or customer-data handling should be reflected on this page and the Subprocessor Register. For questions, use WhatsApp +267 77 723 273 and request an AI/privacy escalation.
