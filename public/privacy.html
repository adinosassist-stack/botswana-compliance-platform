Privacy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. This page is a plain-language summary of Thebe Desk’s public privacy approach and should be read together with any service agreement that applies to your workspace.

Information you provide

Thebe Desk may process information you enter into the service, such as account details, company information, employee or operational records, uploaded evidence and support communications, so the service can provide the features you request.

How information is used

Information is used to operate, secure, support and improve Thebe Desk; provide workspace features; maintain appropriate account and role access; and respond to support or service requests.

Public website and private workspace

The public market page is separate from authenticated workspace content. Private workspace data is not intentionally loaded into the public market page.

Role-aware access

Thebe Desk is designed so authenticated workspace access can be limited according to a user’s role and permissions. Workspace owners remain responsible for deciding who should have access to business information.

Service providers and legal requirements

Technical service providers may process information when necessary to operate Thebe Desk. Information may also be handled where required to comply with applicable legal obligations or valid lawful requests.

Retention and account requests

Retention depends on the type of information, the service relationship and applicable operational or legal requirements. For privacy, access or account-related questions, contact Thebe Desk support through the support channel shown on the main website.

Professional information

Thebe Desk helps organise business information and workflows. It is not a substitute for professional legal, tax, accounting or regulatory advice.
