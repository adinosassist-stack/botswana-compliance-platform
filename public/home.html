Questions

Clear boundaries from the start.

Does the public homepage load my business data?

No. The public site is now a separate surface. Private session and workspace state are loaded only when you open the authenticated app.

Does Thebe Desk replace a lawyer or accountant?

No. It helps organise obligations, evidence and decisions. Professional judgement remains appropriate for legal, tax, accounting and regulated matters.

Can employees see owner information?

Workspace access is role-aware. Employee access is separated from leadership and owner-only surfaces.

What happens if the workspace service has a problem?

The public website remains available independently. Workspace failures should no longer turn the public homepage into a blank or restricted portal.
