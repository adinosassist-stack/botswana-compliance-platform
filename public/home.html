Questions

What buyers usually want to know.

Is Thebe Desk suitable for a small business?

Yes. Monitor starts at P149/month for an owner-managed business, while larger plans add staff, branch and more advanced operating controls.

How does Thebe help with CIPA and BURS?

Thebe helps you organise known obligations, deadlines, records and supporting evidence. It does not replace professional legal, tax or accounting judgement.

How long does setup take?

You can create a trial workspace immediately and start adding the business information, people, locations and records that matter to you.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

You choose the plan that fits the business. No payment card is required to create the trial, and paid activation happens after payment is confirmed.

Can I manage more than one branch?

Yes. Plan limits vary, with Control and higher tiers designed for larger multi-location operations.

Can employees use Thebe?

Yes. Access is role-aware so employees and managers can work in the areas appropriate to their responsibilities without seeing owner-only information.

Can I use WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp is available as an entry point for Thebe. The authenticated workspace remains the system of record for governed business data and actions.

Is my company information private?

The public website does not load your private workspace data. Authenticated workspace access is separated from the public market page and controlled by user role.

Does Thebe replace a lawyer or accountant?

No. Thebe helps organise work, obligations, evidence and decisions. Professional judgement remains appropriate for legal, tax, accounting and regulated matters.

Can I cancel?

Your subscription plan controls paid access. Contact Thebe Desk support if you need help changing or ending a paid plan.
