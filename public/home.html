Questions

What business owners usually want to know.

What does Thebe Desk actually do?

It brings money, work, people and compliance into one operating view, then helps you see what needs attention and keep evidence behind important actions.

Is it built for Botswana?

Yes. Botswana is the live launch market, with workflows around CIPA, BURS, employment, licences and tenders. The core platform is built to expand across Africa.

Does Thebe replace my accountant or lawyer?

No. It helps organise work, evidence and decisions. Professional judgement still belongs with qualified people for legal, tax, accounting and regulated matters.

Can Thebe AI see all my business information?

No. Workspace access is role-aware. Thebe uses only the business context your role is allowed to access, and important actions remain approval-controlled.

Can I manage more than one branch?

Yes. Higher plans support multi-location operations, linked workspaces, branch reporting and group-level oversight.

How do payments and activation work?

The trial needs no card. During launch, you choose a plan, make payment and the paid plan activates after receipt is confirmed.
