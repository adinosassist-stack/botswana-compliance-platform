Privacy Notice

This notice explains the categories of information Thebe Desk may handle, why the service uses it, the controls available to customers, and where public disclosure is still being completed.

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Botswana launch
Transparency note. This notice does not claim a specific data-residency country, certification, or AI provider unless Thebe Desk has publicly verified it. Those items will be published before they are represented as contractual commitments.

1. Scope

This notice applies to the public Thebe Desk website and authenticated Thebe Desk workspace. The public marketing site is designed not to load a customer's private workspace data.

2. Information handled

Account information

  • Name or display name, email address, authentication data and account status.
  • Business or workspace name, plan and role information.

Workspace information

  • Business records customers choose to enter, including operational, employee, compliance, finance-related, branch, task and evidence metadata.
  • Files or evidence customers upload to support business workflows.
  • Audit and activity records needed to preserve accountability and service integrity.

Technical and support information

  • Request identifiers, timestamps, security events and service logs.
  • Messages customers send to support or through public contact channels such as WhatsApp.

3. How information is used

  • Provide and secure the workspace.
  • Authenticate users and enforce role-aware access.
  • Store and retrieve customer-submitted records and evidence.
  • Provide reminders, workflow support, AI-assisted features and customer support where enabled.
  • Prevent abuse, diagnose failures and maintain auditability.
  • Meet lawful retention, dispute, security or legal-hold requirements where applicable.

4. Service providers and subprocessors

Thebe Desk may rely on third-party infrastructure or service providers to operate functions such as hosting, storage, email, security, communications, payments or AI. A dedicated Subprocessor Register is being maintained so customers can see verified providers and purposes. If a provider is not listed there, customers should not infer that provider's involvement from marketing copy.

5. Security

The public implementation shows controls intended to include HTTPS-only production configuration, security headers, rate limiting, role-aware access, private object storage for production evidence, malware-scanning requirements and audit logging. See the Security & Trust page for the current public summary.

6. Retention, export and deletion

Thebe Desk provides account-data export functionality and supports account-deletion requests. Deletion may be delayed where an active legal hold or another lawful retention requirement applies. Retention periods can differ by data category and operational need; specific contractual retention commitments should be documented in the applicable customer agreement or DPA.

7. Customer controls

Workspace owners can use available account controls to export data, request deletion and manage access. Customers may also contact support to request help with privacy, deletion or account-access questions.

8. AI-assisted features

AI features may process information that a user intentionally submits to the AI experience. Provider, retention, model-training and cross-border-processing details must not be assumed. The current public position is documented on AI Data Use.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use Thebe Desk's public support channel and request a privacy escalation: WhatsApp +267 77 723 273.

Thebe Desk will update this notice as verified legal-entity, data-residency, provider and privacy-contact information is formalised for public disclosure.
