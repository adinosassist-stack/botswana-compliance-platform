2. Customer-selected communication channels

Thebe Desk publicly offers WhatsApp as a support and product-entry channel. Information a customer chooses to send through WhatsApp is also subject to the applicable WhatsApp/Meta service terms. The authenticated Thebe Desk workspace remains the intended system of record for governed business information.

3. Changes to providers

Thebe Desk should update this register when it begins using a new material processor for customer workspace information or materially changes the purpose of an existing one. The “Last updated” date will identify the latest public revision.

4. Customer diligence

Customers with contractual residency, sector, confidentiality or cross-border requirements should request written confirmation before relying on an unverified provider or region assumption.
