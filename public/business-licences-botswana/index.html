Botswana licence compliance guide

Business licence compliance in Botswana: know what is due, where it applies and what proves it.

A licence register is useful only if it reflects the real operating business. Thebe Desk connects each recorded licence to the correct company and operating location, validates its dates, and surfaces the next renewal or annual-fee action before a gap becomes operational risk.

Practical boundary: Thebe Desk is compliance-management software. It does not replace current official guidance or professional legal, tax or accounting advice.

Build a licence register that matches the real business

  • Record the licence or registration type, issuing authority, issue date and the operating location it applies to.
  • Keep annual-fee or renewal dates separate from the issue date and reject impossible calendar dates or dates that move backwards in the licence history.
  • If a business operates from more than one location, do not let a licence from one tenant or location be attached to another by mistake.
  • Keep the supporting licence document or payment evidence linked to the control instead of relying on a status label alone.

Annual fees, renewals and “valid indefinitely” licences

  • Some Botswana trade or business registration certificates can remain valid while annual fees still recur. Operationally, that means a business can have a document that looks current but still miss an annual action.
  • The workspace therefore focuses on the next required action, not only on an expiry field. A licence can be “recorded” yet still need an annual fee, evidence refresh or location update.
  • When government guidance changes, the business should verify the current authority requirements before relying on an old anniversary or fee assumption.

How Thebe Desk reduces licence risk

  • Licences are visible to the operational manager role, while sensitive tax and ownership information remains separate.
  • Invalid or malformed legacy dates are treated as unknown rather than silently normalized into another calendar date.
  • Licence events can feed broader risk, inspection and evidence workflows so the business sees the downstream effect of a licence change.

Check current official guidance

Rules and service requirements can change. Before acting on a filing, tax, licence, labour or tender requirement, verify the current official source.
