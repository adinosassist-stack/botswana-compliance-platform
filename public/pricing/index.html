Choose the plan by operating complexity

  • Monitor is designed for an owner-managed formal SME that needs recurring deadlines, company records, licence tracking and evidence integrity.
  • Protect adds stronger people, multi-location and tender-readiness controls for SMEs with employees, licences and more operational activity.
  • Control adds inspection/dispute defence, advanced evidence governance and higher AI allowance for regulated or tender-active businesses.
  • Network supports larger multi-location operations and linked company workspaces. Partner is designed for advisers or service partners managing a broader client portfolio.

AI is metered instead of hidden in the subscription

  • Plans include a defined monthly AI allowance where applicable. Additional AI can be purchased as credit packs, which keeps provider cost visible instead of burying unlimited usage inside the base subscription.
  • AI outputs do not replace professional legal, tax or accounting advice, and high-impact workflows can require human review.
  • External payment-provider charges or separately quoted professional services are not presented as part of the core subscription price.
